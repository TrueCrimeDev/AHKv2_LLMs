# Nex-N2.6 Scores 87 of 103 for 15 Cents

This batch covers Thinking Machines' two new Inkling 2 models and every other general-purpose text model that reached OpenRouter since October 1 at $2 or less per million output tokens. Twenty-two models started and twenty finished. Each ran the same suites as every other entry: [AHK-Eval](post.html?slug=ahk-eval-benchmark)'s 36 functions and 181 hidden cases, [AHK-Contract](post.html?slug=ahk-contract-benchmark)'s 24 class contracts, [AHK-Repair](post.html?slug=ahk-repair-benchmark)'s 30 broken submissions, and the 13-task esoteric-features probe. Each task got one cold call through OpenRouter at temperature 0.2 and one submission, graded by the same parse-then-execute pipeline as the rest of the board.

**Nex-N2.6** leads the batch with 87 of 103 for $0.15, the same total as GPT-6 Luna. Five more score between 80 and 85: Beam 1.0, Particle-1, the October 17 snapshot of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, Inkling 2 Small and Muse Ember. None of the twenty passes every Eval task. Nine score 34 or lower, mostly because their code doesn't load in AutoHotkey v2.

## Method Notes

- **Interpreter.** These entries are graded on the v2.1-alpha.38+Console fork, the same build as this month's Gemini 4 and GPT-6.2 batches.
- **Token caps.** `max_tokens` was 16,000 for Eval, Repair and the esoteric probe, and 20,000 for Contract, the same as every recent entry.
- **Spending cap.** Each model had a $1.50 limit. Two reached it. DaisyRose 27B was then allowed to finish at $2.53. Hy4 was left off the board, for the reason below.
- **Dropped.** Ling 3.2 Flash is only offered as a free endpoint, and its provider rate-limited the run so hard that 25 of 103 calls went through in 100 minutes. Rate-limited calls for the other models were retried until they went through.
- **Not included.** Vision models, routers, task-specific variants such as a legal-domain GLM-5.3 FlashX, and free copies of paid models already in the batch.

## Inkling 2

Thinking Machines' first Inkling solved 22 of the 36 Eval tasks in July. **Inkling 2 Small** solves 34, with 175 of 181 cases, and scores 82 of 103 for $0.41. The July run lost 10 of its 30 Repair calls to provider errors, so its four-suite total isn't a fair comparison. On Eval, where both runs are complete, the gain is twelve tasks.

**Inkling 2 Mini** is much weaker. It has 3.4 billion active parameters against Small's 17 billion, and it reasons at length: 36 of its 103 calls ran into the token cap, and its median call used 5,927 tokens. It solves 13 Eval tasks and fixes 3 Repair items, for 30 of 103.

## Two Models Built on Other Models

According to their OpenRouter listings, Particle-1 is post-trained on DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, and Nex-N2.6 is built on GLM-5.3-Flash.

Particle-1 and the DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 snapshot both score 84, with the same Eval result of 35 tasks and 178 cases. Particle-1 gets there on fewer tokens, a median of 1,717 per call against 3,987, and costs $0.19 against $0.28. Both beat October's DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, which scored 79 and hit the cap on 11 of its 30 Repair calls. The snapshot and Particle-1 each hit it on 7.

Nex-N2.6 passes 35 of the 36 Eval tasks and 23 of the 24 Contract tasks. Its median call is 1,326 tokens, the shortest of the six models that scored 80 or more.

## Why the Bottom Half Doesn't Load

Of the batch's 1,800 Eval, Contract and Repair answers, 473 don't load, and every model has at least one. The most common single cause is the remainder operator. AutoHotkey v2 has none. Percent signs mark dynamic references, and the remainder is `Mod()`:

```ahk
s := n % 60      ; Missing ending "%"
s := Mod(n, 60)  ; loads
```

That accounts for 72 answers from 14 of the 20 models, each stopping at `Missing ending "%"`. Two more habits from other languages are common in the answers that fail. A `for i := 1 to n` loop appears in 43 of them, from 11 models, and `//` comment lines in 38, from 6 models. AutoHotkey comments start with `;`, counted loops are written `Loop n`, and `for` walks an object, as in `for i, v in arr`.

The cap is the other problem at the bottom. Ternary Bonsai 2 9B ran into it on 86 of its 103 calls, LFM3-1.7B on 63, and DaisyRose 27B on 58.

## Hy4: No Answers Before the Cap

Tencent's Hy4 made 47 calls before reaching the $1.50 limit. Every one ran to the token cap, and every token was reasoning, so none of them returned an answer. With no complete run, it isn't on the board. The smaller Hy4 Flash finishes most of its calls and scores 64.

## What the Money Says

The batch cost $9.58, and $1.61 of that went to Hy4's unfinished run. Among the new models, Nex-N2.6 is the pick at 87 for 15 cents, and Particle-1 is next at 84 for 19 cents. The most expensive model, DaisyRose 27B at $2.53, scored 25. Two older models still do better per dollar: GPT-6 Luna matches Nex-N2.6 for $0.05, and Haiku 5.5 scores 89 for the same price. None of the twenty comes near [GPT-6.2 Sol](post.html?slug=november-2026-gpt-6-2), which scored 100 for 33 cents.

*Disclosure: Claude Opus 5.5 ran this batch and wrote this post. Every number comes from the same pipeline that graded the rest of the board: parse validation against the v2.1-alpha.38+Console fork, headless execution, and hidden test cases the models never saw.*
